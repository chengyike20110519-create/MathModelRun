## Render Check

`paper/main.pdf` was generated by `code/build_paper_pdf.py` using ReportLab after the environment blocked MiKTeX `xelatex` and `pdflatex` before a TeX log could be produced. This is a rendering-tool fallback, not a substitution of model results.

The checked PDF SHA-256 is `91c18e225b5d0dd9d0fce577847316409d9345d9492a12f2b0d8280d265b38f7`. It is a 24-page Letter PDF with a text layer, Summary Sheet, Contents, page headers, a two-column body, appendices, and all five figure assets embedded. Text extraction confirmed every page is non-empty and confirmed the final `Page 24 of 24` marker.

Poppler pixel rendering was attempted but blocked by the managed runtime's unwritable fontconfig cache. Therefore this audit does not claim pixel-level inspection or page-boundary inspection; that limitation is recorded in `paper/render_log.json`.

## Scope Limits

- The local CSV and historical problem context are documented, but an official contest prompt original is not stored in this case directory. This is therefore an auditable modeling case, not a claim of a ready-to-submit official contest package.
- ARIMA and Granger results use transparent NumPy fallbacks because the current runtime lacks the usual statistics packages. The Granger figures are exploratory statistics only.
- CO2 and temperature projections are trend extrapolations. They are not physical climate models, confidence intervals, or causal proof.

## Acceptance Decision

All ten structural gates are supported by existing evidence, including the PDF and final-audit gates. The project can enter `READY`; any later work should be a deliberate model or source upgrade rather than a repair of incomplete provenance.
